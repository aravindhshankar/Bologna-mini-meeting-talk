## Key facts
### Motivation (p1)
- Hohenberg–Mermin–Wagner–Coleman: no spontaneous breaking of continuous symmetry in 2D
  classical / relativistic settings. 1D quantum paradigm: Luttinger liquid, log fluctuations of
  a compact phase → power laws, quasi-LRO. Refs: Mermin & Wagner PRL 17, 1133 (1966);
  Hohenberg Phys. Rev. 158, 383 (1967); Coleman Commun. Math. Phys. 31, 259 (1973);
  Halperin J. Stat. Phys. 175, 521 (2019).
- Other routes to 1D U(1) LRO need non-generic ingredients (long-range interactions, fine
  tuning): Maghrebi, Gong, Gorshkov PRL 119, 023001 (2017); Watanabe, Katsura, Lee PRL 133,
  176001 (2024) (frustration-free, ω~k² Goldstone).
- Loophole: the usual argument relies on a quantum→classical map. Ferromagnets/bosons have a
  Berry-phase term, first order in imaginary time → complex Euclidean weight. It makes the
  conserved density canonically conjugate to the compact phase θ.
- Berry term alone is not enough: needs critical softening of the density (inverse uniform
  susceptibility → 0, quadratic cost becomes gradient-like) → zero-point θ fluctuations IR finite
  → true LRO at the QCP.

### Field theory (p2–3; Nahum arXiv:2506.21540 (2025))
- L = i ∂xθ ∂τφ + ½(∂xθ)² + (λ2/2)(∂xφ)² + ½(∂x²φ)² + (λ4/4)(∂xφ)⁴ + …
  θ 2π-periodic, φ 1-periodic; ∂xφ = density / S^z. Tune λ2 >0 → <0 at λ4>0: S^z ∼ ∂xφ
  condenses (Z2 FM). Nahum: U(1) (generated by S^z) broken exactly at the Z2 transition.
- Interacting, non-Gaussian: λ4 relevant. z from ε=2−d expansion: z = 2 − 2ε²/243 ≈ 1.99 at
  ε=1 (Flores-Calderón & Zündel arXiv:2511.09097); numerics Chepiga & Essler arXiv:2609.31319.
- Nonperturbative argument (p2): integrate out θ → K(∂τφ)²; interactions depend only on
  gradients of φ so K is not renormalized → [∂xφ] = (3−z)/2. Unbroken U(1) → conserved
  density has dimension 1 (Fisher–Grinstein–Girvin PRL 64, 587 (1990); Wen PRB 46, 2655
  (1992); Sachdev Z. Phys. B 94, 469 (1994)). Incompatible if z≠1 → U(1) broken.
- Large N (p3–4): N-component θ, φ; λ4/(4N)[(∂xφ)²]²; HS field ψ; critical saddle gives
  g0 = 1/(ω²+k⁴). χθ: ln χθ,β → A (1+O(1/x)) — LRO (source cost β²Λ/2π (1 − sinΛx/Λx)).
  χφ: perturbation about uniform saddle fails at large x; nonlinear saddle; r = 4π²(∂x u)⁴;
  action stat_u ∫[f u − ½(∂τu)² − (2π²/3)(∂xu)⁶]; scaling x'=|x|X, τ'=|β|^{-2/3}|x|^{5/3}T,
  u = |β|^{1/3}|x|^{2/3}U → log χφ,β ∼ −N Cφ |β|^{4/3}|x|^{2/3}. Effective zφ = 5/3.
  Gaussian (zeroth order) would give log χφ = −Nβ²|x|/4 (pure exponential).
  Power counting check: balance fu ∼ (∂τu)² ∼ (∂xu)⁶ → αu = 2/3, ατ = 5/3 (supp. p14, S59);
  the other self-consistent case (terms 2&3) gives x^{1/2}, subdominant.
- Fig. S3 (p17): χθ saturates (~0.9), χφ decays with x^{2/3} fit, β=1, N=1 continuation.

### Lattice model (the user's part; p1, p4–5, supp. p17–22)
- H = −t Σ (c†iσ c_{i+1,σ} + h.c.) + U Σ n_i↑ n_i↓ + Σ V_{σσ'} n_iσ n_{i+1,σ'}.
  V↑↑ = V↓↓ = V++, V↑↓ = V↓↑ = V+-. t = 1, U = 0.1, V++ = 0.8 fixed; tune ΔV = V+- − V++
  (checked on the rendered p4, 2026-11-23; an earlier note had this backwards).
  FM needs opposite-spin repulsion to win: V+- > V++.
  1/4 filling per spin species, k_F = π/4. Symmetry U(1) ⋊ Z2.
- Spin sector maps exactly to the compact Lifshitz theory (K. Yang PRL 93, 066401 (2004));
  also Sachdev & Senthil Ann. Phys. 251, 76 (1996); Kozii et al. PRB 96, 094419 (2017).
- Second-order transition at ΔV_crit ≈ 3.55: paramagnetic LL → FM with finite S^z
  magnetization; ⟨S^z⟩ ∼ |ΔV − ΔV_crit|^0.42 (fit, iDMRG χ=200), Fig. S6 (p20).
  Phase separation on the FM side (Fig. S7), predicted by Nahum. Deep in FM (saturated): LL,
  gapped spin, c=1.
- LL phase checked: density, S^z, S^+ correlators vs bosonization; central charge c = 2.001
  (ΔV=3.0) (Fig. S4, p18).
- Luttinger parameters (perturbative): K_{c/s} = (1 ± Ua/πv_F + 2a(V++ ± V+-)/πv_F)^{-1/2}.
  Identification: φ = φs/√(2π), θ = √(2π) θs, λ2 = (2π/K_s)². (p4)
- Bosonization: c_{i↑} ∼ e^{i(k_F x + √(π/2)(φc+φs+θc+θs))} (Eq. 23, right mover only;
  checked on rendered p4, 2026-11-25).
  S^+_i ∼ e^{-i√(2π)θs}[cos √(2π)φs + cos(√(2π)φc + 2k_F x)] (Eq. 24, checked on rendered p4,
  2026-11-24; the 2k_F part carries φc) → charge LL attached → only QLRO
  even at the QCP, decays ∼ |x|^{-K_c} (Fig. 2, p5). In a pure spin model S^+ would show LRO.
- Order parameter: O_i = S^+_i S^+_{i+1} ∼ e^{-i√(8π)θs} = e^{-2iθ}: |β| = 2 phase vertex, spin-2,
  breaks U(1) → Z2: spin-nematic. LL phase: ⟨O†O⟩ ∼ |x|^{-4/K_s}; K_s diverges at the QCP.
  Derivation (Klein factors, T1+T2) supp. p19 (S87–S88). S^+_i S^-_{i+1} ∼ cos √(8π)φs = conjugate
  vertex |β| = 4π.
- Result: at QCP ⟨S^z⟩ = ⟨S^±⟩ = 0, ⟨S^+S^-⟩ QLRO, but lim ⟨O†_{i}O_{i+x}⟩ > 0: true LRO (Fig. 1 top).
- Electron Green's function C(x) = Σσ⟨c†σ(x)cσ(0)⟩, 4-site rolling average (kills k_F
  oscillations). LL: |x|^{-¼(Kc+1/Kc+Ks+1/Ks)}. QCP: φs vertex shortest-ranged →
  stretched exponential, exponent close to 2/3 (Fig. 1 bottom inset, χ=1200). Fit slope drifts
  towards 2/3 with bond dimension (finite-entanglement scaling, Fig. S8, p21).
- Numerics: finite DMRG ITensors.jl, N = 64, 128, 256, χ=500 (checked to 1200); iDMRG TeNPy,
  4-site unit cell, χ up to 1200; correlations to 0.9 ξ. Argo cluster, ICTP. Data: Zenodo
  10.5281/zenodo.23208777.

### Outlook (p5)
- Simple model → quantum simulators.
- Relation to frustration-free U(1)-breaking models (Watanabe–Katsura–Lee 2024): ω∼k² Goldstone,
  but those have O(L) exact degeneracy vs Anderson tower here.
- Criticality of one order enhancing another; non-Abelian version with WZW terms.
- Note added: Chepiga & Essler found numerical evidence in a spin-1 chain.

## Q&A prep
- "Doesn't Coleman forbid this?" Coleman assumes Lorentz invariance (z=1); the QCP has z≈2 and
  a Berry-phase term, so the Euclidean weight is not a classical Boltzmann weight.
- "Is it fine tuned?" One parameter (ΔV), as for any critical point; not infinitely many.
- "Why not S^+?" Charge LL attached in the itinerant chain → QLRO; nematic O is pure spin.
- "Large N at N=1?" Exponent 2/3 is a large-N saddle result; DMRG exponent is close to 2/3 and
  drifts towards it with χ.
- "Difference from frustration-free models?" Unique ground state + Anderson tower here vs exact
  O(L) degeneracy there.
